Protect yourself from wire fraud

Criminals send fake emails that look like they come from agents, title companies, or lenders, asking you to wire money or change payment instructions. Elite Realty Group will never email or text you wiring instructions. Before you send any money, call your title company at a phone number you’ve verified independently, not one from an email.
