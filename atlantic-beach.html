About Atlantic Beach

Atlantic Beach is the northernmost of Jacksonville’s three beach cities, stretching along the ocean from Atlantic Boulevard up toward Mayport. It’s its own city with its own government, so it keeps a small-town feel while staying close to everything Jacksonville offers.

The south end of town shares the Beaches Town Center with Neptune Beach: a compact, walkable district of shops, restaurants, and courtyards where Atlantic Boulevard meets the ocean. Farther north, Kathryn Abbey Hanna Park offers beach access, trails, and freshwater lakes.
