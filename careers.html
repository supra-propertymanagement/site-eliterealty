Step 2

Two Ways to Join Elite

New to Real Estate

Never held a license? Here’s the path to becoming a Florida sales associate:

  1. Complete the state-approved 63-hour pre-licensing course (available online) and pass the course exam.
  2. Apply to the Florida DBPR and submit your fingerprints.
  3. Pass the Florida real estate sales associate exam.
  4. Affiliate with a broker to activate your license. That’s where we come in.
  5. Finish the 45-hour post-licensing course before your first renewal.

Talk to us early. We’re happy to answer questions while you’re still in class.

Already Licensed
