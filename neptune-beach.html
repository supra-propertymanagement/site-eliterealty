About Neptune Beach

Neptune Beach sits between Atlantic Beach to the north and Jacksonville Beach to the south. It’s the smallest of the three beach cities, with its own city government and a mostly residential feel.

The north edge of town is home to the Beaches Town Center, where Atlantic Boulevard meets the ocean. Locals walk or bike to its shops, restaurants, and events, and the beach is a short stroll from most of the town.
