About Ponte Vedra Beach

Ponte Vedra Beach is an unincorporated coastal community in St. Johns County, about 18 miles southeast of downtown Jacksonville. It’s best known for golf: TPC Sawgrass, home of THE PLAYERS Championship, is here, along with several other golf and country clubs.

South of the main village, the Guana Tolomato Matanzas National Estuarine Research Reserve protects tens of thousands of acres of beaches, marshes, and trails. The result is a mix of oceanfront estates, golf-course communities, and homes along the Intracoastal Waterway.
